# Canvas support

Canvas is an iPadOS photo frame. No login is required for the Apple Photos
workflow.

## Before reporting a problem

1. Confirm that Canvas has access to the intended Photos albums in iPadOS
   Settings.
2. In Canvas, refresh the selected albums and retry playback.
3. For Google Photos, repeat the supported Picker flow and confirm that the
   desired items were selected before tapping Done.
4. Include the iPad model, iPadOS version, Canvas version, and a short list of
   steps that reproduce the problem. Do not include private photos, OAuth
   tokens, or other credentials.

# Canvas Slideshow 1.0.2 (64)

## Change

Keep the battery, date, and clock together in one compact column when weather is enabled. The weather panel no longer introduces a gap between the supporting labels and the clock. The slideshow and Settings preview share the same composition, retaining configured text order and landscape/portrait weather placement.

Canvas privacy policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026.

Canvas is a private iPad photo frame. No account or login is required for the Apple Photos workflow.

What Canvas accesses

  • Only the Apple Photos albums and media you authorize. Canvas does not edit, delete, or upload Apple Photos items.
  • Local settings, exclusions, imported audio, and selected Google Photos media in the app container.
  • Optional Google Photos Picker authorization and the media you explicitly select. OAuth tokens stay in the iPad Keychain.
  • Photo location metadata already present in an authorized photo. When you opt into current weather, Canvas requests When In Use location permission and sends the current coordinates to Apple's WeatherKit. The last weather snapshot is stored locally for an explicitly labelled offline fallback.

Canvas has no Canvas-operated media server and includes no analytics, advertising, tracking, or data brokerage.

Storage and deletion

Disconnect Google Photos to remove its saved authorization. Deleting Canvas removes its local settings, exclusions, imported audio, downloaded Google Photos media, and stored tokens; it does not remove Apple Photos or Google Photos items.

Third parties

Apple system frameworks provide Photos, media playback, Keychain, and local storage. Google receives information necessary for the optional Google Photos Picker and OAuth flow when you choose that feature.

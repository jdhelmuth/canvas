# Foreground album recovery

Canvas preserves unresolved selections and never equates two albums by title. After initial checks at 1, 3 and 8 seconds, foreground retries continue after 30, 60, 120 and then 300 seconds, capped at five minutes. Repeated metadata reconciliation does not restart the backoff. Background/inactive stops the pending timer; foreground refreshes immediately and starts a fresh burst if needed. Denied/restricted access and fully recovered selections stop retries.

A previously recorded local identifier may recover a collection if reverse cloud mapping temporarily fails, but only one compatible candidate with a freshly verified identical cloud identity is accepted. Failed, ambiguous, or different identities remain unavailable. Names alone, including duplicate names, never authorize substitution. Renaming a still-identifiable collection is supported; absent records do not establish deletion. No selection or asset is removed by this recovery process.

The existing shared snapshot remains authoritative for playback and warnings. Unavailable selected albums keep a shuffle cycle incomplete; available photos are not repeated early. Image requests still allow iCloud downloads and use the existing cancellation, timeout, and load-failure handling. Empty real albums and user membership edits retain their existing semantics; Photos can expose an empty readable collection without identifying whether its contents are still syncing. This change does not fabricate cached membership or promise recovery through external outages.

Regression coverage includes actual recovery-runner continuation beyond startup without Photos notifications, idempotent starts, cancellation, resume, stopping after permission loss/resolution, alias recovery, changed titles, ambiguous aliases, wrong cloud identity and Limited Access. The aggregate suite also covers more than 1,000 assets, overlapping albums, load failures, timer edits, relaunch, unavailable album return and cycle boundaries.

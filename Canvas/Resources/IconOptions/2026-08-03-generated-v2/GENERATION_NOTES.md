# Canvas app-icon concepts — second exploration

Generated as five separate calls with the built-in image generator on 2026-08-03. Each source raster was visually inspected, then resized from 1254 × 1254 to an opaque 1024 × 1024 RGB PNG master. The selected third concept, `03-keepsake-stack.png`, is now the exact 1024px `AppIcon` master; the smaller catalog entries are direct raster derivatives for the required iPad sizes.

Every prompt shared these production constraints: exact 1:1 square, full-bleed raster artwork, a simple silhouette that remains legible at small sizes, safe margins for platform masking, no text or letters, no watermark, no device mockup, no camera/lens, no UI, no transparency, and no baked-in iOS rounded-corner mask.

## 01 — Gallery Beam

One luminous square picture plane floating in a vertical shaft of warm gallery light. Deep ink-navy and indigo backdrop; warm-cream frame; abstract apricot-to-coral glow; intimate museum-light mood; refined cinematic 3D materials.

## 02 — Folded Light

One broad sculptural canvas ribbon folding into an open square frame without forming a letter. Full-bleed ivory-to-peach field; deep ink-navy outer face; coral/apricot inner face; editorial paper/fabric rendering; bright sunlit-studio mood.

## 03 — Keepsake Stack

Three thick photo mats in a compact isometric stack, with an abstract sunset field in the top mat. Deep aubergine-to-midnight backdrop; warm-cream edges; tactile matte paper and enamel; intimate shelf-light mood.

## 04 — Color Echo

Exactly three broad concentric rounded-square frames receding toward a coral center. Full-bleed cobalt-to-indigo field; warm-cream middle edge; matte enamel and softly lit glass; centered frontal symmetry; calm visual depth without camera-aperture cues.

## 05 — Canvas Lightwell

A single square recess pressed into a tactile burnt-coral canvas field, with a warm-cream inner rim and a midnight-indigo plane crossed by a low apricot horizon. Restrained shallow relief; architectural and inviting rather than button-like.

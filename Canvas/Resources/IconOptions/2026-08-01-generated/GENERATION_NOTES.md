Generated as five separate calls with the built-in image generator on 2026-08-01. The deliverable PNGs were resized from the generator's 1254 × 1254 output to App Store-ready 1024 × 1024 RGB PNG masters without alpha. They are intentionally not installed in `AppIcon.appiconset`.

Constraints: exact 1:1 square; 1024-style app-icon master; full bleed; no text; no letters; no watermark; no device mockup; no border outside the artwork; no photographic scene; no excessive tiny detail.
